# E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales and profitability data to understand business performance across different product categories, sub-categories, regions, and customer segments.

Using Excel, MySQL, Python (Pandas), and Power BI, the project explores sales trends, profit margins, discount impacts, product performance, and return patterns to identify business opportunities and areas requiring improvement.

The objective is to transform raw transaction data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of transaction data, but understanding sales performance alone is not enough to evaluate overall business success.

A business also needs to understand:

- Which product categories generate the highest sales and profits?
- Which regions contribute the most to overall profitability?
- How do discounts affect sales and profit margins?
- Which products or sub-categories generate losses?
- How do product returns affect business performance?
- Which areas require improvement to increase profitability?

This project addresses these questions through data analysis and interactive dashboards.

## Key Business Insights

The analysis identified several important findings:

- Total sales reached approximately **$2.33 million**.
- Total net profit was approximately **$292.3K**, with a profit margin of around **12.56%**.
- Approximately **1,901 transactions (18.65%)** were identified as loss-making.
- Discounts reaching up to **80%** highlighted the potential impact of aggressive discounting on profitability.
- Technology generated approximately **$839.9K in sales**, making it a major sales contributor.
- The West region contributed approximately **$110.8K in profit**.
- Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies together recorded approximately **$22.6K in losses**.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can help improve business performance:

1. **Optimize Discount Strategies:** Review high-discount transactions and introduce discount limits where necessary to protect profit margins.

2. **Improve Product Profitability:** Investigate loss-making sub-categories such as Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies to identify pricing and cost-related issues.

3. **Focus on High-Performing Categories:** Strengthen strategies for profitable product categories while monitoring their profit margins.

4. **Regional Performance Monitoring:** Analyze differences in regional profitability to identify opportunities for improvement.

5. **Monitor Product Returns:** Track return patterns to understand their impact on revenue and profitability.
